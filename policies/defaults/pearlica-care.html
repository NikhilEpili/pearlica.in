2. Understanding plating — what every customer should know

Plating is a thin decorative layer of metal (rose gold or rhodium) applied over the 925 silver base. It is the industry-standard method used across jewellery brands worldwide. Being informed about how plating works helps you enjoy your jewellery longer and set the right expectations.

How plating behaves over time

  • Plating will gradually fade or thin over time with regular use. This is normal and expected — it is not a defect.
  • The silver base underneath is never affected. Only the top decorative layer changes.
  • High-friction areas (inner ring bands, bracelet clasps, chain links) may show wear sooner than other parts.
  • Colour may shift subtly as plating thins — rose gold may take on a slightly cooler tone; rhodium may show the silver beneath.

Rose gold plating vs rhodium plating

  • Rhodium is harder and more durable than rose gold plating. Quality rhodium plating can last 12–24 months with proper care.
  • Rose gold plating is slightly thinner and softer. It may show wear at friction points after 6–12 months depending on how it is worn.
  • Both finishes benefit significantly from following the care instructions in this policy and on our Jewellery care guide.

What accelerates plating wear

  • Exposure to water, sweat, perfume, lotion, hairspray, or cleaning chemicals
  • Sleeping, swimming, bathing, or exercising while wearing jewellery
  • Storing jewellery without its pouch (scratching and rubbing between pieces)
  • Skin that is naturally more acidic or oily may experience faster wear
  • Applying perfume or lotion after wearing jewellery (always apply before, let it dry, then wear)

Following the care instructions in Section 5 will significantly extend the life of your plating.

4. Pearlica anti-tarnish guarantee

All Pearlica jewellery — rose gold plated and rhodium plated — comes with a 6-month anti-tarnish guarantee from the date of delivery.

What tarnish is: Tarnish is a natural reaction where silver reacts with sulphur compounds in the air, causing darkening. It is not a defect. Plating delays tarnishing; once plating wears thin, tarnish may appear.

Our anti-tarnish guarantee covers the jewellery’s performance under proper care — not misuse or neglect.

What the guarantee covers

  • Premature or excessive tarnishing within 6 months of delivery, where the piece has been stored and cared for as instructed in Section 5

What the guarantee does not cover

How to claim the anti-tarnish guarantee

5. Care instructions — how to keep your jewellery looking its best

Following these instructions protects your plating, extends jewellery life, and keeps your warranty and guarantee valid. For more detail, see our Jewellery care guide.

Daily care

  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after every use
  • Always put jewellery on last — after perfume, lotion, hairspray, or makeup
  • Remove all jewellery before washing hands, bathing, swimming, or any water activity
  • Remove jewellery before exercising, cooking, or activities involving sweat or heat
  • Avoid prolonged contact with sweat — remove and wipe clean after long wear

Storage

  • Store each piece separately in its sealed anti-tarnish pouch
  • Keep in a cool, dry place away from humidity, sunlight, and extreme temperature
  • Do not store jewellery in the bathroom or near windows where moisture is present

Cleaning

  • Clean gently with a soft dry cloth only — no abrasive materials
  • Do not use chemical jewellery cleaners, toothpaste, or baking soda on plated jewellery
  • Do not use ultrasonic or steam cleaners — these damage plating

Failure to follow these care instructions may void the plating warranty and anti-tarnish guarantee. When evaluating claims, our team may ask about storage and usage conditions.
